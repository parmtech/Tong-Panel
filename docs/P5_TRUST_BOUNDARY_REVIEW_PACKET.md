## Required review annotations for every confirmed flow

For each flow, reviewers should record:

1. Confirmed source, destination, direction, boundary, and initiating principal (including asynchronous workers and service identities).
2. Data classes crossing the boundary: tenant/customer data, identifiers, credentials or secret references, signed artifacts, policy/version data, operations, results, and audit/events as applicable. For each class, record its sensitivity and confidentiality/integrity/availability impact, plus required handling and lifecycle (access, storage/transit, retention, deletion, and redaction) without assuming an unapproved classification policy.
3. The authenticated identity, tenant/resource binding, authorization decision point, relevant policy/revocation version, and execution-time recheck requirements; identify the source of truth and its owner.
4. Trust assumptions, abuse cases, and failure behavior for stale, replayed, missing, malformed, compromised, or partitioned participants.
5. Candidate mitigations and the evidence needed to verify them, linked to threat IDs and P5 acceptance scenarios. Label unverified requirements as required future evidence, never as completed controls.
6. Residual risks, affected upstream owner/gate, named decision owner, review disposition, and follow-up issue. Silence or a passing offline model is not acceptance.
